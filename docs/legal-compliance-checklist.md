# SKULL — Legal publishing checklist

Reviewed 2026-10-30. The public site is a portfolio and email contact channel; it does not accept payments or online service orders.

## Published information
- Spanish: `/legal/`, `/privacidad/`, `/terminos/`, `/aviso-legal/`, `/creditos/`.
- English: `/en/legal/`, `/en/privacidad/`, `/en/terminos/`, `/en/aviso-legal/`, `/en/creditos/`.

- Sitemap includes both locales. SEO canonicals and alternates use the existing locale-aware layout.
- Source law links: Ley 25.326, AAIP personal-data rights, Ley 24.240, Ley 11.723, Disposición 954/2025.

## Confirm before claiming legal compliance
1. The public-facing name **SKULL** is not enough to establish the legal identity of a service provider in all contexts. Confirm the actual operator details, whether commercial/fiscal identification and a registered address need to be disclosed, and whether the website is personal or business-operated. Do not publish anyone's unverified personal information.
2. Confirm the real Cloudflare configuration, analytics, server-side logs, security tools, third-party integrations and any cookie use. Adjust privacy copy if services change.
3. Confirm ownership and permission for each public asset, commercial screenshot, logo and generated illustration. Keep attributions/licensing current.
4. If online purchases, subscriptions, online contracting or payment processing are added, review consumer-protection disclosures and applicable **Botón de Arrepentimiento** and **Botón de Baja** requirements under Disposición 954/2025. The current contact-only portfolio does not offer a paid online checkout.
5. If a CRM, newsletter, form backend or analytics is connected, assess lawful basis, retention, disclosures, processors, transfers, and consent controls as applicable under Ley 25.326.
6. Obtain Argentina-specific professional legal review before declaring the content fully compliant.

This is a factual checklist, not a certificate of compliance. Avoid fake terms of sale, fictitious registered companies, fabricated fiscal identifiers and unsupported fixed retention periods.
